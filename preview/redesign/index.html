Structural resilience under extreme hazards

SHEAR Lab (Structures, Hazards, Evaluation, And Resilience) develops advanced technologies to enhance structural resilience under extreme hazards, contributing to a safer, more resilient world through research and innovation in hazard mitigation.

Steel & hybrid structural systems

Composite and hybrid (steel, timber, concrete) components, seismic performance, carbon-neutral design.

Large-scale experimental testing

Component- and system-level testing for seismic performance evaluation and model validation.

LiDAR, computer vision, and foundation-model-based monitoring for digital twin applications.

Multi- and many-objective optimization for seismic design and retrofit/repair decision support.
